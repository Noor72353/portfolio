About Me

I am a BS Computer Science graduate from the Federal Urdu University of Arts, Science & Technology (FUUAST), Islamabad, Pakistan, with a CGPA of 3.75/4.00 and hands-on experience developing AI and computer vision systems.

My technical experience includes Python, Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Machine Learning, Image and Video Analysis, Multimodal Interaction, and desktop application development. I am particularly interested in building practical intelligent systems that combine perception, interaction, and real-time analysis.

My research interests include Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, 3D Vision, Multimodal AI, HCI, Intelligent Systems, and Speech and Vision-Based Interaction.

I have developed VisionSlide, my Final Year Project, an offline gesture and voice-controlled presentation system, and an AI-Based Real-Time Video Analytics System for object detection, multi-object tracking, counting, line-crossing, zone, dwell-time, and trajectory analysis.

I am interested in pursuing graduate study, research, and practical opportunities in AI, Computer Vision, Machine Learning, Multimodal AI, HCI, and Intelligent Systems.
